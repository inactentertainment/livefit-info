PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

LiveFit is designed to keep the free-tool experience lightweight. Most assessment and calculator data is stored locally in your browser unless you choose a feature that requires sending information to a service provider.

Last updated October 5, 2026

Information stored on your device

LiveFit uses browser storage to remember your theme preference, assessment answers, calculator results, generated workout settings, equipment matches, and roadmap preview status. This information can be cleared with the “Reset My LiveFit Data” control.

Information you may provide

If you enter an email address during the roadmap checkout flow, it is used for the roadmap purchase and delivery process once payment is enabled. During pre-launch QA, the checkout flow may save testing preferences only on your device.

Payments

When payment is activated, card or payment-account details will be handled by the selected payment processor. LiveFit will not intentionally store full card numbers or security codes on this static website.

Analytics and third-party content

Embedded videos and future analytics or affiliate services may receive technical information such as device/browser data under their own privacy policies. We aim to minimize unnecessary tracking.

Affiliate links

Some future gear recommendations may use affiliate links. Clicking an affiliate link may allow the merchant or affiliate network to attribute a purchase to LiveFit.

Your choices

You can use most free tools without creating an account. You can clear locally stored LiveFit data at any time. Do not enter information you do not want stored on the device you are using.

Changes

This policy may be updated as payment, email delivery, analytics, or account features are activated. Material changes will be reflected on this page.
