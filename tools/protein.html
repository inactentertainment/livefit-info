HOW IT WORKS

A practical range, not one magic number.

1

Start with body weight

Protein needs are often estimated relative to body weight, then adjusted for training and goals.

2

Match the goal

Resistance training and muscle-building goals generally support a higher target than basic wellness.

3

Spread it out

Dividing protein across several meals can make the daily target easier to reach.

This calculator gives general educational estimates for adults. People with kidney disease, pregnancy, certain medical conditions, or clinician-directed diets should get individualized advice before making major protein changes.
