01

Steps are only one measure.

Walking pace, hills, duration, and how often you move during the day also matter. A smaller repeatable target can be more useful than chasing one universal number.

02

Your stride changes the math.

LiveFit estimates stride length from height and sex used for the calculation. Real stride varies, so distance is an estimate rather than a GPS measurement.

03

Build gradually.

If your current average is low, adding a modest amount first may be easier to sustain. Increase gradually as walking feels comfortable.

HOW THE ESTIMATES WORK

Simple math, personalized by your inputs.

1

Distance

Steps are multiplied by an estimated stride length, then converted from inches to miles.

2

Time

Estimated distance is divided by your selected walking speed to estimate time.

3

Calories

Walking calories are estimated from body weight, time, and a standard activity-intensity value for the selected pace.

This is a general wellness tool, not a medical prescription. If you are new to exercise, have significant mobility limitations, cardiovascular symptoms, or pain that worsens with walking, get appropriate medical guidance before increasing activity substantially.
