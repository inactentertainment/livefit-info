HOW IT WORKS

Useful estimate, not a perfect measurement.

1

BMR

We estimate basal metabolic rate using the Mifflin–St Jeor equation.

2

Activity

Your BMR is multiplied by an activity factor to estimate total daily energy expenditure.

3

Adjust

Real calorie needs vary. Track weight, hunger, energy, and performance and adjust gradually.

This tool is for general educational use. Calorie needs can differ substantially with pregnancy, medical conditions, medications, aging, athletic training, or a history of disordered eating. For individualized nutrition advice, consult a qualified health professional.
