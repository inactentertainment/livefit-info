Exercise risk

Physical activity carries risk. Start conservatively, use appropriate equipment and technique, and stop if you experience chest discomfort, fainting, severe shortness of breath, sharp or worsening pain, or another concerning symptom. Seek appropriate medical attention when needed.

Nutrition estimates

Calorie and protein estimates are not appropriate for every person. Pregnancy, kidney disease, eating-disorder history, medications, medical conditions, athletic training, and other circumstances can substantially change appropriate targets.

Inspect equipment before use and follow manufacturer instructions. Space-planning and equipment-matching results are approximate and do not replace structural, electrical, flooring, clearance, or manufacturer requirements.
