ABOUT LIVEFIT

Fitness that fits real life.

LiveFit.info is built for adults who want useful fitness guidance without turning health into a full-time job.

What LiveFit does

LiveFit combines practical calculators, workout generators, walking and nutrition tools, equipment guidance, long-form articles, videos, and a personalized roadmap experience. The emphasis is on helping adults—especially people in midlife and older adulthood—make clearer decisions about strength, movement, conditioning, recovery, and everyday fitness.

Who it is for

The site is designed broadly for adults, with special attention to the realities that often matter more after 40: maintaining strength, staying mobile, protecting independence, managing recovery, building useful cardio fitness, and creating routines that can actually be repeated.

Technology with a human direction

LiveFit uses modern digital tools, including human-directed AI, to help organize information, personalize experiences, generate interactive tools, and support creative media. Technology is used to make the experience more useful and accessible—not to replace appropriate professional judgment or medical care.

Free tools + optional personalized roadmap

The calculators and fitness tools remain available independently. The personalized roadmap is an optional one-time purchase that organizes a user's saved answers and tool results into a printable action plan. Pricing is shown before purchase and there is no recurring LiveFit membership charge.

Built by InAct Entertainment

LiveFit.info is a project of InAct Entertainment LLC. The broader InAct ecosystem combines education, digital media, interactive tools, music, AI-assisted production, and practical consumer resources.
